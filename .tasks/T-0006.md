# TRZ-0006 — Grid snap / alignment snapping on the canvas

_Generated by `kdb`. Do not edit by hand._

Cards don't snap to line up with each other when dragged. Want Figma/Spatial-style alignment: edges and centers snap to nearby shapes' edges/centers with guide lines while dragging. tldraw has built-in snapping (hold cmd while dragging, or editor.user isSnapMode preference) — first try enabling isSnapMode: true so snapping is the default without a modifier; if the built-in behavior isn't right, look at custom snap geometry on the ShapeUtil. Also decide whether a coarse grid (isGridMode + gridSize) is wanted on top of shape-to-shape snapping.
