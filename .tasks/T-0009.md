# TRZ-0009 — Replace the video VID badge with a centered play overlay

_Generated by `kdb`. Do not edit by hand._

On canvas video cards, remove the textual VID badge. Show a compact play icon centered over the poster frame inside a subtle circular background instead. The overlay should remain legible across light and dark posters, avoid obscuring too much of the thumbnail, scale appropriately with the card, and expose an accessible video label without relying on the visible VID text. It is an affordance indicating video content; normal card activation behavior remains unchanged.
